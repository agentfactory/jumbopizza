## ORDER FLOW
1. Ask what they'd like to order
2. For pizza: confirm size (small/medium/large/XL), crust type, stuffed crust option, confirm toppings
3. For wings: confirm quantity and sauce choice(s)
4. Ask for any additional items
5. Confirm pickup or delivery
6. Get customer name and phone number
7. If delivery: get full address
8. Any special instructions?
9. Read back the complete order with prices and total (including 13% HST)
10. Confirm and say estimated time
